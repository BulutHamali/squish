How to Compress Images Online

Compressing an image reduces its file size so it loads faster, uses less storage, and meets upload size limits — without visibly degrading the image at typical viewing sizes.

  1. Upload your image — drag and drop or click to browse. Squish accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP files up to 20MB.
  2. Choose your settings — use the quality slider to balance size vs. quality, or switch to Target Size mode and enter an exact size limit.
  3. Click Compress — compression happens instantly inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  4. Download your file — your compressed image is ready.

JPG vs PNG — Which Should You Compress?

FormatBest forCompression typeTypical reduction
JPG Photos, product images, social media Lossy (quality adjustable) 60–90%
PNG Screenshots, logos, transparent images Lossless only 10–40%
WebP Web images (modern browsers) Lossy or lossless 25–35% vs JPG

For the best results: use JPG at 70–80% quality for photos. Convert PNG photos to JPG for massive size reductions. Keep PNG for images with transparency or crisp edges.

Why Squish Doesn't Upload Your Files

Most online image compressors upload your image to their servers, compress it there, and send it back. Squish uses your browser's built-in Canvas API to compress images entirely on your device. The image data never leaves your computer. No account needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Squish really not upload my files?

Yes, really. Squish uses the browser's Canvas API to process images entirely in memory on your device. Disconnect from the internet after the page loads — it still works.

How much can I reduce an image's file size?

For JPG photos, reductions of 60–90% are typical with minimal visible quality loss at 70–80% quality. A 3MB phone photo often compresses to under 300KB.

What's the difference between Quality mode and Target Size mode?

Quality mode lets you set a specific quality level (1–100%). Target Size mode is the reverse: enter a maximum size (e.g. 100KB) and Squish automatically finds the highest quality that fits.

Can I compress PNG files without losing quality?

PNG is lossless — you can't reduce its size by lowering quality. Convert to JPG (if no transparency needed) or WebP for meaningful compression.

What's the maximum file size?

Squish supports files up to 20MB. Processing happens in your browser so very large files may take a few seconds on older devices.

Is Squish free to use?

Yes, completely free. No account, no sign-up, no watermarks, no limits.
